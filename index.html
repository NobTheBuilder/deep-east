About

Deep East is a live electronic music project from Goa, India. Drawing on the free-flowing, time-bending concepts of eastern musical traditions, the artist seeks a form of flow in electronic music — built on a hypothesis of ancient Indian musicians having discovered electronic instruments centuries ago. This is his ongoing story — his dance with the God of Time ···.

Pain is Information, Patience is Currency.

Acceptance is Expansion, Arrogance is Saturation.
